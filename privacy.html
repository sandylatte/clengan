Privacy

Clengan · last updated 22 September 2026

Clengan does not collect anything. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Your financial records never leave the device you typed them into, because there is nowhere for them to go.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter — transactions, accounts, categories, budget settings — is stored in your browser's own storage (IndexedDB) on your device. Your chosen colour theme is stored the same way. None of it is transmitted.

What is sent over the network

The app's own files, once, when you install or update it. That is all. Clengan makes no request to any third party: the spreadsheet library, the animation library and both fonts are bundled with the app rather than fetched from anyone else's server. There are no trackers, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting and no usage statistics.

After the first load the app works fully offline.

Exporting to Excel

The Backup feature writes a spreadsheet to your device when you ask it to. That file is yours and goes only where you send it. Clengan does not upload it anywhere, and cannot read it once it has been written.

Because your data exists only on your device, an export is the only backup that exists. If you clear the browser's site data, uninstall the app, or lose the device, the records go with it.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app, or clear site data for it in your browser settings. That removes everything immediately and permanently. Nobody needs to be asked, because nobody else has a copy.

Children

Clengan is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version adds optional accounts or sync, that version will collect an email address and store encrypted data on a server, and this page will be updated before it ships. The version described here does neither.
